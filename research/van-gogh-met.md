## Notes on Data Source

The Met's object-search endpoint returned HTTP 410 (endpoint decommissioned) at the time of research. The object records were retrieved directly by their Met object IDs (436535 and 436532) via the object-detail endpoint, and the department was cross-checked against the museum's official department list (European Paintings, Department ID 11).
